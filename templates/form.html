Share a mental health tip

Your tip will be reviewed and, if approved, posted anonymously to our Slack channel. No one — not HR, not engineers, not me — can tell who wrote it.

What this page does and doesn't do

  • Does not ask for your name, email, or any identifier.
  • Does not set cookies, run analytics, or load anything from outside this server.
  • Does not log your IP address in this app, or store anything that could link the tip to you.
  • Does save: the tip text, the category, and a timestamp rounded to the hour.
  • Cannot edit or delete your tip after you submit — we have no way to know which one is yours.

Honest caveat: our hosting provider sees standard web request metadata (including your IP) for a short retention window, the same as any website you visit. Our app never reads or stores it. The source code is open at github.com/awburgard/mental-health-tips so you can verify.

Before you submit — please read

This form is not monitored for HR, legal, or emergency support, and it is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care.

If you have a workplace concern, please reach out directly to your manager or HR so we can address it through the proper channels.

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis or urgent safety issue, please contact emergency services (911) or a crisis resource such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or other local emergency/mental health services.

While this space isn't intended for reporting issues, if something is shared here that indicates harassment, discrimination, safety concerns, or risk of harm, we may still need to follow up through our normal HR and safety processes.

Thanks for keeping this a respectful and supportive space.
